\subsubsection{Follow-up on the Restricted Benchmark}
Notebook replication reproduced the ridge and prototype-consistency results on Kaggle. A separate
Degformer run that excluded training rows touching the outer-test protein clusters completed all
three seeds and five folds. Its ensemble AUROC was \nRoneAuc{}, compared with \nAucRidgeExt{} for
the extended-training ridge. This is distinct from the earlier one-seed, saturation-only retraining:
the clean-cluster comparison has now been performed, without establishing a transformer advantage.

The frozen ESM2-650M/PCA ridge comparison reached \nRtwoAuc{}, but its paired interval against
ridge included zero. In the subsequent representation and context study, ridge with an ordered
$\pm5$ context reached \nRsixWfive{}, with an adjusted interval of \nRsixWfiveCI{} for the AUROC

comparison with ridge had interval \nRsixPrimaryCI{} and did not meet its confirmation criterion.
These findings motivated the matched pointwise/CNN control in the full-PSI study.

\subsubsection{Full-PSI Development Evaluation}\label{sec:res-fullpsi}
\paragraph{Population and protocol.}
The full-PSI follow-up retains \nVthreeNPeptides{} saturation peptides, \nVthreeNCells{} mutation
cells and \nVthreeNGroups{} protein groups after technical quality control, without removing
peptides by their WT stability. The supporting scanning bundle contains \nVthreeNScanCells{} cells.
The label remains the replicate-mean $\Delta$PSI from the Zhang reporter assay
\citep{zhang2023,zhang2023data}; these are reprocessed public measurements, not new wet-lab data.
Groups combine whole-proteome cov80 clusters, additional bidirectional cov50 similarity hits and
exact-sequence unions. This is heuristic homology control, not a proof of biological independence.
Scanning data are partitioned by group before fitting into development, held-source and
saturation-overlap subsets; overlapping scanning groups do not supply transfer-training labels.

Five outer folds and seeds 17, 29 and 43 share the evaluation cells. Training is either restricted
to $\mathrm{PSI}_{\mathrm{WT}}\le4$, uses the full range, or uses the full range balanced first by
WT-PSI stratum and then by peptide. Inner validation covers the full range in all three arms;
therefore the restricted arm is not entirely unexposed to high-PSI labels during model selection.
Scaling, PCA and hyperparameter selection remain inside the applicable training split. This protocol
was designed after earlier results were known and is an extension for development, not an independent
preregistration. It defines a new evaluation population rather than expanding the old 91-peptide test.

\paragraph{Models and metrics.}
Ridge with ordered $\pm5$ context remains the reference. Frozen ESM2 representations
\citep{lin2023esm} are compared through pointwise, single-window and multi-window convolutional heads.
For the matched ESM comparison, the crop, auxiliary inputs and projection are held common; the
pointwise head does not mix positions, whereas the CNN does. This isolates positional mixing more
closely than comparing different pLM pooling schemes. It does not make the parameter counts equal.
Neural epochs are chosen by inner MSE before refitting on the permitted outer-training data.

The primary measure is mean absolute error in PSI units, calculated within each peptide and then
averaged across all \nVthreeNPeptides{} peptides. Positive-event AUROC uses
$\Delta\mathrm{PSI}\ge1$ and requires at least three cells in each class; only
\nVthreePlusEligible{} peptides qualify. High-PSI peptides can still inform regression when a positive
event is physically unavailable. Each table entry averages the three seed predictions per cell
before calculating metrics; it is not an average of seed-level metric values.

\paragraph{What the results establish.}
Full-range training reduces W5 ridge MAE from \nVthreeRidgeB{} to \nVthreeRidgeD{}
(\nVthreeRidgeGain\%), with the largest gains in the high-WT-PSI strata. Balancing lowers MAE
further to \nVthreeRidgeE{}, but does not preserve positive-event ranking as well; it is not a
uniform improvement. The adjusted intervals in Table~\ref{tab:v3-comparisons} support a reduction
in MAE, but their upper endpoints do not establish the prespecified minimum reduction of 0.02 PSI
throughout the intervals.

Frozen ESM2-35M with a CNN reaches MAE \nVthreeCnnD{}. Relative to the matched pointwise head,
the difference is \nVthreeCnnDelta{} with descriptive interval \nVthreeCnnCI{}.
The balanced multi-window CNN has the lowest selected MAE, \nVthreeMcnnE{}, but its advantage
over the full-range single-window CNN is only \nVthreeMcnnGap{} PSI. The ranking alone does not
establish an architecture winner. Choosing the top 5\% of predicted positive substitutions within
eligible peptides gives macro precision \nVthreeCnnTopFive\% for the ESM CNN; this is a ranking
utility measure, not a calibrated probability or whole-dataset accuracy.

\paragraph{Domain and software integration.}
The earlier domain-gate study completed both tiers: OOF AUROC was \nRthreeLookupAuc{} using
screen PSI and \nRthreeSequenceAuc{} using sequence-derived features. This estimates membership
of the restricted baseline's training domain, not per-mutation prediction accuracy. Raw screen PSI
and intrinsic WT PSI are different estimators, so applying the intrinsic threshold directly to
screen PSI is invalid. The baseline service still requires the delivered gate adapter and its
borderline state. A future full-PSI serving model would need its own domain and calibration policy.

The local React/FastAPI baseline, reference ETL and recorded baseline tests exist. The newer
workspace interface is an interactive design prototype with synthetic scores and tab-memory
state; it does not establish backend inference or durable storage. Projects/history, peptide and
protein jobs, experimental import/comparison and local PostgreSQL acceptance remain open.
The serving model remains the calibrated restricted-PSI ridge baseline; none of the new research
scores constitutes automatic model promotion or completion of the research and software gates.
