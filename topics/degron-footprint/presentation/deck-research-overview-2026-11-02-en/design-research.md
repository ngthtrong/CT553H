# Design research and English edition

This is the English edition of the faculty research briefing. It preserves the visual direction selected for the Vietnamese edition after the original template/style survey.

| Reference | Design principle used |
|---|---|
| [Penn State Assertion–Evidence](https://writing.engr.psu.edu/assertion_evidence_EA.html) and [checklist](https://www.writing.engr.psu.edu/AE_checklist.pdf) | Short messages supported by visual evidence; detail remains in speaker notes. |
| [Harvard Catalyst: scientific slides](https://catalyst.harvard.edu/writing-communication-center/visualize-science/slides/) | Audience-oriented scientific explanations and visual examples. |
| [Slidev Academic](https://github.com/alexanderdavide/slidev-theme-academic) | Academic hierarchy and presentation structure; the default theme is not used. |
| [EMBL design guidance](https://www.embl.org/guidelines/design/design-guidelines/colours/) | Life-science greens and generous white space; no EMBL logo or claim of affiliation. |

The English edition uses the same light background, forest-green emphasis, charts, original diagrams, looped process animations and motion controls. Labels are translated before diagrams are wrapped and scientific figures are regenerated from the same JSON values. Decimal points are used in English. People retain their original names.

Figures remain standalone SVG artifacts. The HIF1A map uses measured labels. The Fam188a GIF illustrates protein folding, not a measured degron structure. The interface screenshot is an English design prototype using synthetic fixtures, explicitly labelled on the slide.

The original survey and design decisions remain available in the [Vietnamese edition](../deck-research-overview-2026-11-02/design-research.md). Sources and conditions are in Notes, the reference appendix and evidence files rather than footnotes on each slide.
