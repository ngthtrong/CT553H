Introduction · 2 of 4 · why degradation matters

Protein level is set by a balance: synthesis against degradation

A protein's concentration in the cell is not set by production alone.

amount in cell = rate of synthesis − rate of degradation

Cells destroy proteins on purpose, and constantly:

  • to switch signals off on schedule — cell-cycle regulators, transcription factors
  • to clear misfolded or damaged copies before they aggregate
  • to adjust quickly to changed conditions
Consequence. A protein that is made correctly but destroyed too fast is just as absent as one that was never made. Degradation is a control layer in its own right.

Related work · 1 of 5 · the precondition for reading the next table

How degron data is measured: the GPS stability assay

One experimental idea behind every dataset here — Global Protein Stability (GPS) profiling, Koren et al., Cell 2018.

StepWhat is done
1peptide library, each fused to a GFP reporter
2one peptide per cell, second reporter as control
3FACS sorts cells by reporter ratio into bins
4sequence the bins ⇒ one stability score per peptide
The resulting label — Protein Stability Index (PSI)
1 — low PSI
degraded quickly = strong degron 6 — high PSI
stable = not a degron

Scale 1 … 6 (6 bins) in the dataset used here.

Mutation effect = a difference between two measured constructs
ΔPSI = PSI(mutant) − PSI(wild type)

Keep the sign straight: low PSI = degraded, ΔPSI > 0 = rescued. Same direction in every figure here.

Gap · 1 of 2 · the strongest asset of this thesis

Today's best model cannot say which residue matters

The closest published model, PAP, cannot answer the residue-level question — a property of the model class, not of its training.

In plain words
What it seeswhich amino acids are present, not where they sit
What followsthe same substitution gets the same predicted effect at every position
Does it matter? We checked the measured data

In 9 of 10 measured peptides, the same amino-acid change stabilises one position but not another — a model that only counts amino acids cannot tell them apart.

Nguồn và chú thích của slide:
PAP is not a bad model — it was trained for a segment-level score. Using it for residue-level effects is using it outside its design.

Appendix A5 · the measurement behind the provable gap, and the Degformer audit

A5 · PAP architecture and the permutation test

Why PAP cannot localise — the argument in full
  • PAP's internal channel is Conv1D(kernel=1) → GlobalAveragePooling, so its score is a function of amino acid composition only
  • the predicted effect of a substitution is Δf(a→b) = f(c − ea + eb) − f(c) — no position index on the right-hand side
  • ⇒ substitutions sharing an amino acid pair receive identical predictions, whatever their positions
  • Permutation test, own measurement: shuffling residue order leaves the score unchanged — Pearson 1.000000 between original and shuffled predictions
  • on the scanning set, 2,353 of 2,624 footprint peptides (89.7%) have a pair group holding both a positive and a non-positive position; the corresponding figure measured on the full scanning set in an earlier session was 68.9%

PAP is not a bad model — it was trained for a segment-level score. Using it per residue is using it outside its design.

Degformer — audit of the closest precedent
  • no publication; assessed by reading the full source and its shipped CSV
  • random train/test split with 4.09% identical-sequence leakage
  • duplicate sequences kept with conflicting labels
  • the head reads the CLS token only; the 28×20 matrix is assembled from 532 forward passes, not from a residue-level head
  • blind test set absent from the repo ⇒ self-reported 0.9162–0.9463 is not reproducible
  • checkpoints are pickles — loading one executes arbitrary code ⇒ isolated directory, python3 -I
The task is therefore not unattempted — a 28×20 matrix has been output before. What is missing is the evaluation condition: a protein-cluster split, a measured label ceiling, and per-prediction uncertainty.
Phụ lục A5

⚠ Tuyệt đối không nói "chưa sản phẩm nào xuất ma trận 28×20". Cách nói đúng: nhiệm vụ không còn trống, điều kiện đánh giá còn trống.

5. Dòng "ranking reverses by metric" phải giữ. Tự nêu thì mạnh: cùng một mô hình thắng ở thang mức vị trí và thua ở thang mức ô.
