- Source: [the authors' PDF hosted by Timms Lab](https://www.timmslab.com/media/papers/2023_Mol_Cell_Elucidation_of_E3_ubiquitin_ligase_specificity_through_proteome-wide_internal_degron_mapping_naf6vNE.pdf).

- A historical repository note reports different license information; reuse is based on the license declaration in this specific PDF. That historical note was not modified.
- The paper has a Figure 2A erratum, DOI 10.1016/j.molcel.2023.10.021. Figure 2A is not used in this deck.
