## 09. PSI is a reporter index on a scale from 1 to 6

build_psi.py normalizes counts by bin and computes a weighted mean of bin indices. p(b) is the normalized proportion of a construct in bin b, summing to 1; PSI=sum b*p(b). Higher PSI indicates a more stable reporter within this assay. This is not a splicing PSI or a generic protein-stability measure.

## 10. WT and mutant measurements define ΔPSI

Saturation has two halves, sat_a and sat_b. Each WT reference is the median of valid synonymous cells in its own half. Mean ΔPSI = mean(PSI_mut_a−WT_a, PSI_mut_b−WT_b). For R1A, Δa=0.289803860 and Δb=0.471006817; mean=0.3804053385. PSI(WT)=4.0427815905 and mean mutant PSI=4.423186929. This observation does not validate an E3 mechanism.

## 17. Full-PSI training retains technical quality control

Reads≥10. Saturation requires at least 10 valid synonymous cells per half; each half uses its own WT median, then matched mutation cells are averaged across halves. Scanning uses the correct replicate-specific _mut0 and retains rep1/rep2/mean12 separately; BAG6 WT is not pooled. Seventeen unmapped scanning peptides are excluded; 45 saturation peptides missing legacy groups are mapped using the full proteome. See the data-method audit and the loader/builder code.
