\paragraph{Contribution assessment.}
The verified contributions are a traceable label/QC and grouped-evaluation workflow, compatible
baseline and adaptation comparisons, retained unsuccessful controls, and an explicit account of
uncertainty and transfer limits. These are methodological and empirical contributions within the
studied reporter assay. They are not evidence that combining a pLM with convolution or adapters is
itself a new architecture. The software contribution is a working baseline and an expanded design;
implementation and acceptance of the expanded workflows remain separate milestones.

\paragraph{Next experiments and decision criteria.}
The highest scientific priority is a new validation design with a compatible endpoint, audited
protein/sequence overlap and an explicit source history. Freeze the candidate recipe and analysis
plan before inspecting its outcome; do not reuse the inspected T set to select among candidates.
Report peptide/group-macro regression, positive and negative ranking, large-effect residuals,
WT-PSI strata and probability reliability together. A favourable ranking score alone is insufficient
to claim accurate effect sizes or calibrated probabilities. Further capacity or transfer experiments
should specify matched controls and computational budgets. Final serving refit and export should
preserve the chosen ensemble and calibration policy, or re-evaluate them when changed. These are
proposed steps; no new training or validation experiment was executed to update this report.
