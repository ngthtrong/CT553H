\FrontChapter{ABSTRACT}

A degron is a sequence element that marks a protein for selective degradation, and a single amino acid
substitution within it can change its activity. Experiments score whole peptides, while measuring every
substitution is costly. This thesis studies how to predict the effect of single amino acid substitutions in
degron peptides at residue level from experimental stability data, evaluates generalisation with
protein-cluster-aware splits against ridge, PAP-difference and Degformer baselines, and builds a prototype
tool that displays predicted effect matrices, ranks substitutions and exports results with provenance.
