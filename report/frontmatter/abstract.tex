\FrontChapter{ABSTRACT}

A single amino acid substitution can alter the activity of a degron, a sequence element involved in
selective protein degradation. This research progress report, dated 7 November 2026, examines
substitution effects as changes in the Protein Stability Index ($\Delta$PSI) measured by a peptide
reporter assay and the development of a local analysis tool.
The computational work reconstructs labels, controls sequence overlap and evaluates models with
protein-group-aware folds, inner model selection and group-bootstrap confidence intervals.

The full-PSI development set contains \nVthreeNPeptides{} peptides, \nVthreeNCells{} mutation cells
and \nVthreeNGroups{} protein groups. Expanding ridge training from wild-type (WT) PSI at most 4 to the full
range reduces peptide-macro mean absolute error (MAE) from \nVthreeRidgeB{} to \nVthreeRidgeD{} PSI.
A completed local study compares nine frozen protein language models and 60 matched fits with
and without low-rank adaptation (LoRA). LoRA reaches \nCampaignLoraB{} MAE with restricted
training and \nCampaignLoraD{} with full-range training; group intervals support both gains over
matched frozen controls. Scanning-label pre-training helps its tested convolutional control,
whereas quality weighting does not help ridge. Calibration improves Brier score but retains errors.

A retrospective out-of-fold replay selects ten mutations per peptide under the same
\nPrioritySelections{}-selection budget. Positive Precision@10 is \nPriorityLoraPrecision\% for
LoRA-D, \nPriorityWfivePrecision\% for W5-D and \nPriorityRandomPrecision\% for random
expectation. LoRA-D recovers \nPriorityExtraHits{} more measured hits than W5-D; its
\nPriorityDeltaWfive{}-percentage-point gain has adjusted 97.5\% interval
$\nPriorityWfiveCI{}$. Both primary reference contrasts favour LoRA-D within this replay.

On a locked same-laboratory scanning set, W5 reaches positive area under the receiver operating
characteristic curve (AUROC) \nCampaignTAuc{}, but
its MAE advantage over zero is unresolved; LoRA has not been tested there. A React/FastAPI
baseline exists; expanded persistent workflows remain incomplete and the serving model is
unchanged. Results remain development evidence: neither retrospective selection counts nor
633 completed campaign jobs establish prospective wet-lab yield, laboratory cost savings,
independent external, intact-protein or clinical validation.
