\FrontChapter{ABSTRACT}

A single amino acid substitution can alter the activity of a degron, a sequence element involved in
selective protein degradation. This research progress report, dated 6 November 2026, examines
substitution effects as changes in the Protein Stability Index ($\Delta$PSI) measured by a peptide
reporter assay and the development of a local analysis tool.
The computational work reconstructs labels, controls sequence overlap and evaluates models with
protein-group-aware folds, inner model selection and group-bootstrap confidence intervals.

The full-PSI development set contains \nVthreeNPeptides{} peptides, \nVthreeNCells{} mutation cells
and \nVthreeNGroups{} protein groups. Expanding ridge training from WT PSI at most 4 to the full
range reduces peptide-macro mean absolute error from \nVthreeRidgeB{} to \nVthreeRidgeD{} PSI.
Frozen ESM2-35M with a convolutional head reaches \nVthreeCnnD{} MAE. A completed local study
compares nine frozen pLM checkpoints and 60 matched frozen/LoRA fits. LoRA reaches
\nCampaignLoraB{} MAE with restricted training and \nCampaignLoraD{} with full-range training,
improving on matched frozen controls by \nCampaignGainB\% and \nCampaignGainD\%.
Protein-group intervals support both reductions, while performance by WT-PSI stratum shows a
training-coverage trade-off. Scanning-label pre-training helps its tested CNN control; quality weighting
does not help ridge. Out-of-fold LoRA probabilities improve Brier score but retain calibration errors.

The local campaign ends with 633 completed jobs and two pending input-dependent extensions.
On a locked same-laboratory scanning set, the fixed W5 ridge model reaches positive AUROC
\nCampaignTAuc{}, but its MAE advantage over predicting zero is unresolved; LoRA has not been
evaluated on that set. As of 6 November 2026, a React/FastAPI baseline exists, while persistent
projects, batch/protein jobs and experimental comparison remain incomplete. The serving model is
unchanged and requires a compatible final refit/export before promotion. The results remain assay
development evidence, without independent external, intact-protein or clinical validation.

\par\vspace{6bp}\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} degron, protein stability, mutation effect prediction, ubiquitin--proteasome system, machine learning.
