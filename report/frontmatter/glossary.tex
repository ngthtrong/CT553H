1 & Degron & Sequence element that marks a protein for selective degradation. \\ \hline
2 & E3 ligase & Enzyme that recognises substrate proteins and transfers ubiquitin to them. \\ \hline
3 & Residue & One amino acid at a specific position of a protein or peptide. \\ \hline
4 & Footprint & Map of positions or substitutions that affect degron activity under a given assay. \\ \hline
5 & GPS & Global Protein Stability profiling, a fluorescent-reporter assay of protein stability. \\ \hline
6 & PSI & Protein Stability Index, a summary of the reporter signal distribution over sorting bins. \\ \hline
7 & $\Delta$PSI & PSI of the mutant minus PSI of the wild type. \\ \hline
8 & WT & Wild type, the unmutated reference sequence. \\ \hline
9 & PAP & Model that predicts degron activity of a whole peptide. \\ \hline
10 & AUROC & Area under the receiver operating characteristic curve. \\ \hline
11 & MMseqs2 & Software for sequence search and clustering. \\ \hline
12 & Cov80 / cov50 & Protein clusters built with 80\% / 50\% alignment coverage. \\ \hline
13 & pLM & Protein language model pretrained on amino acid sequences. \\ \hline
14 & LoRA & Low-rank adaptation of selected pretrained weight matrices. \\ \hline
15 & OOF & Out-of-fold predictions made on groups excluded from model fitting. \\ \hline
16 & MAE / MSE & Mean absolute error / mean squared error; regression loss measures. \\ \hline
17 & AP & Average precision, a summary of precision--recall ranking. \\ \hline
18 & ECE & Expected calibration error, summarised over probability bins. \\ \hline
19 & UPS & Ubiquitin--proteasome system, the route that tags proteins with ubiquitin and degrades them in the proteasome. \\ \hline
20 & Ub & Ubiquitin, the small protein attached to substrates as a degradation signal. \\ \hline
21 & FACS & Fluorescence-activated cell sorting; in GPS it sorts cells into six bins by the GFP/DsRed ratio. \\ \hline
22 & Tile Tile & A 28-residue peptide fused to the reporter; also the unit scored by the application. A 28-residue peptide fused to the reporter; also the unit scored by the application, called a window when a whole protein is cut into consecutive tiles. \\ \hline
23 & ESM-2 & A family of transformer protein language models; the 35M-parameter version is used for LoRA. \\ \hline
24 & CNN / mCNN & Convolutional neural network / multi-scale CNN with parallel kernel widths. \\ \hline
25 & W / T & Development set (full-PSI saturation peptides) / held-source scanning set scored once after refit. \\ \hline
26 & Arm B / D & Training restricted to WT PSI at most 4 / training on the full WT-PSI range. \\ \hline
27 & Brier score & Mean squared difference between predicted probabilities and observed events. \\ \hline
28 & Platt scaling & Logistic mapping from a raw score to an event probability, fitted on held-out predictions. \\ \hline
29 & R3 gate & Two-tier domain gate that labels a tile in, borderline or out of the training domain. \\ \hline
